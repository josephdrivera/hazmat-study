## Overview

This chapter covers hazardous materials release control, confinement, containment, defensive strategies, product control equipment, diking and damming, absorbents, leak control concepts, and operational safety.

## Quick notes

### Important Definitions

- Confinement: Keeping released material within a defined area.
- Containment: Stopping material from escaping its container.
- Diking: Creating barriers to restrict liquid movement.
- Damming: Restricting liquid movement along a channel or path.
- Absorption: Taking up a material into another substance.
- Adsorption: Material adhering to a surface.

### Response Considerations

- Identify the substance and its hazards.
- Evaluate potential consequences.
- Determine available resources and competencies.
- Use appropriate PPE.
- Select only tactics authorized for the responder's training level.
- Evaluate whether the action is controlling the hazard.

## Key terms

- **Confinement** — Keeping released material within a defined area.

## Exam takeaways

Understand the differences between containment and confinement, and recognize the limits of defensive versus technician-level product control.
