## Overview

This chapter covers hazardous materials response responsibilities, Awareness and Operations training, hazardous materials laws and standards, OSHA requirements, NFPA competencies, the Authority Having Jurisdiction, and responder qualifications.

## Quick notes

- Hazardous materials incidents require careful assessment before action.
- The material, release conditions, and environment affect the response.
- Responders must work within their training and assigned responsibilities.
- Awareness-level personnel focus on recognition, notification, isolation, and initial protective measures within their scope.
- Operations-level responders may perform trained defensive actions.
- Mission-specific activities require additional competency and authorization.
- Hazardous materials technicians have further specialized responsibilities.

### Important Terms

- AHJ: Authority Having Jurisdiction.
- OSHA: Occupational Safety and Health Administration.
- HAZWOPER: Hazardous Waste Operations and Emergency Response.
- NFPA 470: Hazardous materials/WMD response competencies and qualifications in the supplied newer material.
- EPCRA: Emergency Planning and Community Right-to-Know Act.
