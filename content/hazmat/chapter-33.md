## Overview

This chapter covers risk assessment, incident objectives, response planning, protective actions, isolation and evacuation, shelter-in-place, and choosing response strategies.

## Quick notes

- Responder safety is the first priority.
- Protect threatened people while avoiding unnecessary exposure.
- Evaluate the material, quantity, conditions, and possible release behavior.
- Determine whether evacuation or shelter-in-place is appropriate.
- Follow incident command and department procedures.
- Response strategies may be offensive, defensive, or non-intervention.
- Continuously evaluate changing conditions.

## Exam takeaways

Develop response objectives based on actual conditions, available resources, and responder qualifications.
