## Overview

This chapter covers hazardous materials containers, bulk and nonbulk packaging, fixed-facility storage, transportation containers, container failures, release hazards, chemical and physical threats, and potential criminal or terrorist use.

## Quick notes

- Container size, shape, construction, and pressure may provide clues about possible hazards.
- Pressurized containers may present additional hazards if damaged or heated.
- A release can create fire, explosion, toxic, corrosive, or environmental hazards.
- Recognize that secondary hazards may be present.
- Do not assume a container identifies its contents without reliable supporting information.
- Use placards, labels, shipping papers, SDS, and other appropriate resources.

## Exam takeaways

Understanding the container and material helps responders anticipate possible consequences.
