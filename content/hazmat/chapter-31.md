## Overview

This chapter covers chemical properties, physical properties, vapor behavior, flammability, corrosivity, water reactivity, toxic exposure, and Safety Data Sheets.

## Quick notes

### Important Definitions

- Flash point: Temperature where enough vapor is produced for momentary ignition.
- Fire point: Temperature where sustained combustion occurs.
- Boiling point: Temperature where a liquid boils.
- Vapor density: Density of a gas or vapor compared with air.
- Specific gravity: Density of a liquid or solid compared with water.
- Water solubility: Ability of a substance to dissolve in water.
- Corrosivity: Ability to cause chemical damage to materials or tissue.
- LEL: Lower Explosive Limit.
- UEL: Upper Explosive Limit.

### Routes of Exposure

- Inhalation
- Absorption
- Ingestion
- Injection
