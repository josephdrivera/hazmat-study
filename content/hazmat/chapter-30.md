## Overview

This chapter covers scene size-up, recognizing hazardous materials, hazardous materials containers, placards and labels, shipping papers, Safety Data Sheets, the Emergency Response Guidebook, and identification numbers.

## Quick notes

- Approach suspected releases from a safer direction, generally upwind, uphill, and/or upstream.
- Recognize hazards from a safe distance.
- Look for placards, labels, container shapes, and visible evidence of a release.
- UN/NA identification numbers are commonly four digits.
- Shipping papers help identify hazardous materials being transported.
- SDS documents provide information about chemical hazards and handling.
- Use the ERG as an initial transportation incident reference.
- Do not approach or contact unknown substances without appropriate training and protection.

## Exam takeaways

Recognize and identify hazards before selecting protective actions.
