## Overview

This chapter covers scene management, incident action plans, hazard control zones, personnel accountability, incident communications, protective actions, decontamination, and ongoing assessment.

## Quick notes

- Scene size-up is continuous.
- Scene control helps maintain safety and accountability.
- Establish hazard zones appropriate to incident conditions.
- Hot zone: Area of significant hazard or contamination.
- Warm zone: Area commonly associated with decontamination and hot-zone support.
- Cold zone: Area outside the established hazardous and contamination-reduction zones.
- Control entry and exit.
- Coordinate activities through the incident command structure.
- Reassess the effectiveness of the response.

## Key terms

- **Hot zone** — Area of significant hazard or contamination.
- **Warm zone** — Area commonly associated with decontamination and hot-zone support.
- **Cold zone** — Area outside the established hazardous and contamination-reduction zones.

## Exam takeaways

Implement the incident action plan safely while monitoring conditions and controlling exposure.
