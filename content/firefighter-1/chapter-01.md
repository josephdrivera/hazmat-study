## Overview

This chapter covers the history and mission of the fire service, community risk reduction, fire department organization, codes, standards, and regulations, policies, SOPs, and SOGs, fire service ranks, types of fire companies and apparatus, and Firefighter I and II responsibilities.

## Quick notes

- Primary mission: Save lives.
- Additional missions: Protect property and the environment.
- Fire suppression: Activities involved in controlling and extinguishing fires.
- 1678: First paid fire department in Boston.
- 1736: Benjamin Franklin organized a volunteer fire company in Philadelphia.
- NFPA: Develops fire safety codes and standards.
- ICC: Develops model building and fire codes.
- SOP: Standard Operating Procedure.
- SOG: Standard Operating Guideline.
- PIO: Public Information Officer.
- ISO: Incident Safety Officer.
- IC: Incident Commander.

### Five E's of Community Risk Reduction

1. Education
2. Enforcement
3. Engineering
4. Economic incentives
5. Emergency response

### Fire Company Types

- Engine company: Water supply and fire attack.
- Truck company: Ladders, ventilation, forcible entry, and rescue support.
- Rescue company: Search and rescue.
- Wildland company: Vegetation fires.
- HazMat company: Hazardous materials incidents.

### Important Apparatus

- Pumper: Pump, water tank, and hose.
- Quint: Pump, tank, hose, aerial device, and ground ladders.
- Water tender: Transports water.
- Aerial apparatus: Provides elevated access and other aerial capabilities.

### Firefighter Qualifications

- Firefighter I: Direct supervision.
- Firefighter II: General supervision.
- NFPA 1010: Firefighter professional qualifications.
