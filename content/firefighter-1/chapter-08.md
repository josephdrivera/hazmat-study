## Overview

This chapter covers rope materials, rope construction, life safety rope, utility rope, knots and hitches, hoisting equipment, and rope care and inspection.

## Quick notes

- General-use life safety rope: Used for appropriate rescue operations.
- Utility rope: Used for tools and equipment, not life safety.
- Static rope: Lower elasticity.
- Dynamic rope: Greater elasticity.
- Kern: Inner rope core.
- Mantle: Protective outer sheath.
- Working end: Rope end used to form knots.
- Standing part: Portion of rope not being manipulated.
- Bowline: Creates a fixed loop.
- Clove hitch: Useful for securing rope around certain tools.
