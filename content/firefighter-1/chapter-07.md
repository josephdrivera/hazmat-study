## Overview

This chapter covers hand tools, cutting tools, power tools, equipment inspection, tool maintenance, tool selection, and safe tool operation.

## Quick notes

### Important Tools

- Ventilation saw: Creates roof ventilation openings.
- Diagonal cutter: Cuts wire and small cable.
- Rabbit tool: Hydraulic forcible-entry spreading tool.
- Closet hook: Short hook used in tight spaces.
- Hacksaw: Saw with blade in a U-shaped frame.
- Cutting torch: Cutting tool that presents ignition hazards.
- Halligan: Multipurpose forcible-entry tool.
- Flathead axe: Cutting and striking tool.
