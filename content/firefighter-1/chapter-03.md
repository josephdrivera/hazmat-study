## Overview

This chapter covers structural firefighting PPE, protective coats, pants, helmets, gloves, boots, and hoods, SCBA components, respiratory protection, PPE inspection, cleaning, and maintenance, and air management.

## Quick notes

- PPE reduces exposure to hazards but does not eliminate risk.
- SCBA supplies breathable air independently of the surrounding atmosphere.
- SCBA harness: Frame and straps securing the apparatus.
- Closed-circuit SCBA: Rebreather.
- Supplied-air respirator: Receives air from a remote source.
- PASS: Personal Alert Safety System.
- Protective hoods protect the head and neck.
- Clean and inspect PPE according to manufacturer and department instructions.
- Skip breathing is a breathing technique described in the chapter; it does not replace safe air management.

## Key terms

- **SCBA harness** — Frame and straps securing the apparatus.
- **Closed-circuit SCBA** — Rebreather.
- **Supplied-air respirator** — Receives air from a remote source.
- **PASS** — Personal Alert Safety System.

## Exam takeaways

Know PPE components, respiratory protection differences, SCBA operation, and equipment limitations.
