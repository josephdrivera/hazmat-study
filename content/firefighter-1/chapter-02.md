## Overview

This chapter covers firefighter health and wellness, physical fitness, injury prevention, scene safety, accountability, incident safety responsibilities, and accident and near-miss investigations.

## Quick notes

- Firefighting requires physical and mental readiness.
- Accidents, injuries, and near misses should be investigated.
- Firefighters must recognize and report unsafe practices.
- Personnel accountability systems track members, assignments, and activities.
- Firefighters should maintain crew integrity.
- Hydroplaning occurs when water separates tires from the road surface.
- Apparatus should stage away from violent scenes until law enforcement declares them safe.
- Safety officers monitor incident hazards and unsafe operations.

### Important References

- NFPA 1851: Protective ensemble care and maintenance, as named in the exam.
- NFPA 1010: Firefighter professional qualifications.

## Exam takeaways

Firefighter safety requires fitness, risk awareness, accountability, safe operations, and reporting of unsafe conditions.
