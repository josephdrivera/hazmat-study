## Overview

This chapter covers fire dispatch, radio communications, emergency communications, Mayday procedures, LUNAR, communications centers, and telephone and digital communications.

## Quick notes

- Dispatch: Select, alert, and communicate with responding units.
- FCC: Federal Communications Commission.
- Simplex: Radio communication in one direction at a time.
- ANI: Automatic Number Identification.
- VoIP: Voice over Internet Protocol.
- Time marks: Periodic incident-time notifications.
- Mayday: Emergency request for firefighter assistance.
- Evacuation signal: Warns firefighters to withdraw.

### LUNAR

- L: Location
- U: Unit
- N: Name
- A: Air supply or Assignment
- R: Resources needed
