## Overview

This chapter covers fire hose construction, attack and supply hose, hose couplings, nozzles, hose appliances, hose maintenance, and hose damage.

## Quick notes

- Attack hose: Delivers pressurized water onto a fire.
- Supply hose: Carries water toward a pumper or other supply point.
- Collapsible hose: Lays flat when unpressurized.
- Swivel gasket: Seals a threaded hose connection.
- Shut-off valve: Controls flow at a nozzle.
- Hose roller: Protects hose moving over edges.
- Mechanical damage: Includes abrasive damage caused by grit.
- Bresnan distributor: Device used to apply water into difficult-to-access spaces.
