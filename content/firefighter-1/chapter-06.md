## Overview

This chapter covers structural systems, building materials, roof construction, building construction types, structural loads, fire resistance, and collapse hazards.

## Quick notes

- Heat reduces the strength of structural materials.
- Gravity acts on structural members and can contribute to collapse.
- According to the supplied chapter, steel loses approximately half its strength at 650°F.
- Heavy timber construction uses large wooden posts and beams.
- Rafter: Inclined structural roof member.
- Flat roofs have a slight slope for drainage.
- Cape Cod houses often contain knee walls and concealed spaces.
- Lightweight structural assemblies can present significant collapse hazards.
- Firefighters must continually evaluate structural conditions.

## Key terms

- **Rafter** — Inclined structural roof member.

## Exam takeaways

Know how buildings are constructed and how structural components respond to fire.
