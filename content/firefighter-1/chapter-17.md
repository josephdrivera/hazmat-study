## Overview

This chapter covers primary search, search priorities, search methods, victim location, search ropes, thermal imagers, victim carries and drags, and search safety.

## Quick notes

- Primary search: Rapid search for people in immediate danger.
- Search where victims are at greatest risk.
- Maintain crew integrity and communication.
- Identify secondary escape routes.
- Search rope: Helps maintain orientation and a return route.
- Tether line: Short line attached to a main search rope.
- Oriented search: Team leader maintains orientation as other members search rooms.
- Stack effect: Vertical airflow associated with indoor/outdoor temperature differences.
- Blanket drag: Used when the victim's clothing is unsuitable for a clothes drag.

### Three Fireground Priorities

1. Life safety
2. Incident stabilization
3. Property conservation
