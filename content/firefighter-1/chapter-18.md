## Overview

This chapter covers property conservation, salvage operations, overhaul operations, hidden fire detection, fire scene hazards, and evidence preservation.

## Quick notes

- Salvage: Reduce damage to property and contents.
- Overhaul: Locate and extinguish hidden fire.
- Secondary damage: Damage from smoke, water, debris, and suppression activities.
- Floodlight: Broad illumination.
- Polyethylene film: May be used as protective sheeting.
- Floor runners: Protect flooring.
- Thermal imager: Helps locate hidden heat.
- Fire investigators should be considered before disturbing potential evidence.

## Key terms

- **Salvage** — Reduce damage to property and contents.
- **Overhaul** — Locate and extinguish hidden fire.
- **Secondary damage** — Damage from smoke, water, debris, and suppression activities.
- **Floodlight** — Broad illumination.
- **Polyethylene film** — May be used as protective sheeting.
- **Floor runners** — Protect flooring.
- **Thermal imager** — Helps locate hidden heat.

## Exam takeaways

Salvage limits additional damage, while overhaul helps prevent rekindling. Both require attention to safety.
