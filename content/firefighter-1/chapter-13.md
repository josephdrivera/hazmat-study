## Overview

This chapter covers hose deployment, hose loading, forward hose lays, reverse hose lays, attack line advancement, and water supply operations.

## Quick notes

- Flake out: Arrange hose for easy advancement.
- Forward lay: Water source toward the fire.
- Reverse lay: Fire toward the water source.
- Minuteman hose load: Allows a firefighter to release hose folds while advancing.
- Gated wye: Splits flow into separately controlled outlets.
- FDC: Fire Department Connection.
