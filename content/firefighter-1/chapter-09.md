## Quick notes

### Starter Facts from the Midterm

- Class A: Ordinary combustibles.
- Class B: Flammable liquids and gases.
- Class C: Energized electrical equipment.
- Class D: Combustible metals.
- Class K: Cooking oils and fats.
- Class K extinguishers are commonly used in commercial kitchens.
- Sodium bicarbonate is an original dry-chemical extinguishing agent.
- Water mist extinguishers are typically white.

## Key terms
