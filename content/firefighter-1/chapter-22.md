## Overview

This chapter covers the Incident Command System, chain of command, incident management, ICS organizational positions, resource assignments, and command transfer.

## Quick notes

- ICS: Incident Command System.
- NIMS: National Incident Management System.
- FEMA administers NIMS.
- Chain of command: Orderly reporting and authority structure.
- Incident command must be established at every incident.
- Incident safety officer monitors incident safety.
- Strike team: Resources of the same kind and type working together under a leader.
- Task force: Combination of resources assembled for a specific assignment.
