## Quick notes

- Swinging doors are a common pedestrian door type.
- Mortise lock: Lock assembly fitted into a cavity within a door.
- Tempered glass: Safety glass commonly used in storefronts and sliding doors.
- Door frame: Housing surrounding the door opening.
- Mineral-core door: Type of solid-core fire-resistant door.
- Irons: Halligan and flathead axe combination.
