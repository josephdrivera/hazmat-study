### Additional terms

- Grid arrangement: Water can reach an area through multiple paths.
- Hydrant barrel: Upright hydrant body.
- Dry-barrel hydrant: Drains to reduce freezing risk.
- Friction loss: Loss of pressure caused by water movement resistance.

### Hydrant Color Classifications

- Red: Less than 500 GPM.
- Orange: 500–999 GPM.
- Green: 1,000–1,499 GPM.
- Blue: 1,500 GPM or more
