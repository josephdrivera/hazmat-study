## Overview

This chapter covers firefighter emergencies, Mayday procedures, lost and trapped firefighters, escape techniques, rapid intervention, downed-firefighter rescue, and breathing-air emergencies.

## Quick notes

- LUNAR: Location, Unit, Name, Air/Assignment, Resources.
- RIC: Rapid Intervention Crew/Company.
- DRD: Drag Rescue Device.
- EBSS: Emergency Breathing Safety System.
- Safe haven: Temporary refuge from danger.
- Backhanded swim: Escape method for passing through a narrow wall opening.
- SCBA straps can assist with firefighter removal.
- Personnel accountability is essential during emergencies.

## Key terms

## Exam takeaways

Recognize emergencies early, communicate clearly, manage air supply, and maintain escape options.
