## Important terms

- **Exposure** — Contact with a hazardous material.
- **Contamination** — Presence of an unwanted hazardous substance.
- **Decontamination** — Process of removing or reducing contamination.
- **Secondary contamination** — Contamination transferred from a contaminated source.
- **Toxic products of combustion** — Hazardous substances formed during burning.

## Physical and chemical properties

- Vapor pressure
- Vapor density
- Specific gravity
- Water solubility
- Corrosivity
- Flash point
- Fire point
- Ignition temperature
- LEL and UEL

## Exposure limits

Do not treat exposure limits as universal safe-entry thresholds. Their use depends on the chemical, duration, conditions, and applicable guidance.
