## Overview

The combined class handouts include an eight-step response process. These activities may overlap and continue throughout an incident rather than occurring in a strictly linear order.

## The eight steps

1. Site Management and Control
2. Identifying the Problem
3. Hazard and Risk Evaluation
4. Protective Clothing and Equipment
5. Information Management and Resource Coordination
6. Implementing Response Objectives
7. Decontamination
8. Terminating the Incident
