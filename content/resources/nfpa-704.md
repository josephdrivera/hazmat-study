## Diamond colors

- **Blue** — Health.
- **Red** — Flammability.
- **Yellow** — Instability/reactivity.
- **White** — Special hazards.

## Numerical hazard ratings

Numerical hazard ratings run from 0 to 4, with 4 the most severe.

## NFPA 704 and DOT placards

NFPA 704 fixed-facility hazard identification is different from DOT transportation placards.
