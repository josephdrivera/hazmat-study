## Purpose

The ERG assists responders during the initial phase of hazardous materials transportation incidents.

## ERG sections

- **White** — General instructions, safety, identification charts, and reference information.
- **Yellow** — Materials listed by four-digit UN/NA identification number.
- **Blue** — Materials listed alphabetically by name.
- **Orange** — Initial response guides, hazards, and emergency response information.
- **Green** — Initial isolation and protective action distance tables and related information.

## Study sequence

1. Identify the material using reliable information.
2. Find the appropriate ERG entry and guide.
3. Read the appropriate orange response guide.
4. Use the green tables when indicated.
5. Follow incident command and the appropriate protective-action guidance.

## Important notes

- ERG guidance is primarily for initial transportation incident response.
- It does not replace training or chemical-specific technical references.
- Do not invent isolation distances.
- Do not calculate protective action distances from memory.
- Use the actual guide tables and incident circumstances.
- The uploaded edition is ERG 2024.
