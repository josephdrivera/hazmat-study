## Overview

This chapter covers the purpose of fire investigation, fire origin and cause, evidence recognition, evidence preservation, chain of custody, and firefighter responsibilities during investigations.

## Quick notes

- Firefighters may observe conditions that help establish a fire's origin and cause.
- Preserve the scene when an investigation is required.
- Avoid unnecessarily disturbing potential evidence.
- Fire investigators conduct formal investigations.
- Chain of custody documents evidence handling and transfer.
- Firefighters should follow investigator and department instructions.

## Exam takeaways

Recognize and preserve potential fire evidence without compromising scene safety.
